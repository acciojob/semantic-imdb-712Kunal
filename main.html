summary

Drishyam is a suspense thriller about Vijay Salgaonkar, a simple cable TV operator, who goes to extraordinary lengths to protect his family after they become involved in an unexpected crime. Using his intelligence and movie knowledge, Vijay creates a perfect alibi to outsmart the police.

Actors

Ajay Devgn plays Vijay Salgaonkar, Tabu plays IG Meera Deshmukh, Shriya Saran plays Nandini, and Ishita Dutta plays Anju.
